    Good to Know

    Before you sit down.

    A few straight answers about chairs, Sundays and what comes with the service.

    Ask on WhatsApp
    Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

    Walk-ins are welcome Monday to Saturday, 8:00 am to 8:00 pm. If you want a specific barber — or you're coming with friends — reserve on WhatsApp so your chair is held.

    How do Sunday bookings work?

    Sundays are strictly by appointment only, and the latest Sunday appointment must be booked at least one day in advance. Message 0741 578791 and the lounge will confirm your slot.

    Can I combine several services in one visit?

    Yes — that is exactly how the menu is built. Select any combination of the 25 services and your total chair time is added up for you before you reserve. The VIP Collection already bundles the full ritual.

    What does the complimentary offer include?

    Every service includes a complimentary aftershave hand & shoulder massage, plus coffee, tea and light bites in the lounge — normally KSh 250, KSh 200 and KSh 350, on the house.

    How does “Bring 2 friends” work?

    Book the group of three (90 minutes, KSh 3,000). Bring two friends, pay for two haircuts, and yours is free. Students with a valid ID also get the “Shave Na Mbogi” package for three at KSh 2,000.

    Where exactly is the lounge?

    Superior Center, Kenyatta Avenue, 4th Floor, Suite K-5, Nairobi. Google Plus Code PR8F+H4 Nairobi — take the lift to the fourth floor and follow the signage.
