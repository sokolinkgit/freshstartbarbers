About Fresh Start Barbers

Pure Craftsmanship. Zero Rush.

Fresh Start Grooming Lounge is a dedicated Nairobi crew that cuts, shaves, sculpts and restores modern gentlemen — executives, grooms, students, Nairobi regulars, international guests and every walk-in in between.

Unlike a rushed street barbershop, we consult before we cut. You stay in complete control of your look — while our five craftsmen deliver calibrated clippers, straight-razor discipline, hot towels and lounge hospitality on the house.
